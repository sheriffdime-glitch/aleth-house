A letter from the house

Kept, not cleaned.

There is a moment, in the last hour of light, when a well-kept machine stops being transportation and becomes something else. The paint deepens. The chrome warms. Every hour of care it was ever given shows up at once.

ALETH exists for that moment.

We are people who love timeless vehicles, and we believe keeping one is a quiet craft. Not a chore. Not a weekend obligation. A craft, passed between those who notice the difference between washed and kept.

So we make chemistry worthy of the machines it touches. Every formula tested on real paint, real wheels, real leather before it earns a name. Every claim we print traceable to a document, filed and published as each formula ships. We left everything else out. The noise, the exaggeration, the costume.

What remains is what was always there. Truth in every drop.
