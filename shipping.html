Policies

Shipping, stated plainly.

Effective October 10, 2026 · Version 2.0

Shipping a precision chemical formulation is not the same problem as shipping a t-shirt, and pretending otherwise is how bottles arrive broken, leaking, or worse. This policy states what we do, where we ship, how fast, what it costs, and exactly what happens when something goes wrong. Every rule here has a reason behind it, and we give you the reason — because you deserve the reason, not just the rule.

Article 1Definitions

1.1"ALETH," "we," "us" means ALETH Chemical Co., Charlotte, North Carolina.

1.2"Customer," "you" means the person or entity that places an Order with ALETH and, where the context requires, the person to whom the Order is addressed.

1.3"Product" means any chemical formulation blended, manufactured, or sold by ALETH and offered for sale through alethchemical.com.

1.4"Order" means a request to purchase Product submitted through alethchemical.com and accepted by ALETH.

1.5"Business Day" means Monday through Friday, excluding United States federal holidays.

1.6"Ground Service" means surface transportation by commercial carrier within the continental United States.

1.7"Contiguous United States" means the 48 lower states and the District of Columbia — excluding Alaska, Hawaii, and U.S. territories.

1.8"Carrier" means the commercial ground carrier to which ALETH tenders a package for delivery.

1.9"Delivery" means the date the Carrier's tracking record shows the package delivered to the address on the Order.

1.10"Limited Quantity Shipment" means a consumer-scale chemical shipment moving under the limited-quantity exceptions of the U.S. Department of Transportation hazardous materials regulations (49 CFR 173.150).

1.11Where a period in this policy is stated in days without the word "Business," those are calendar days. Where the word "Business" appears, only Business Days are counted. Every deadline in this policy is stated in one form or the other on purpose; the distinction is not decoration.

Article 2Where We Ship

2.1ALETH ships to addresses within the Contiguous United States only.

2.2We do not currently ship to Alaska, Hawaii, U.S. territories, or international destinations.

2.3Why, because you deserve the reason: our Products are regulated chemical goods. International movement runs under entirely different dangerous-goods regimes (air and sea), with different classification, packaging, documentation, and duties — and several countries require a locally responsible legal entity for chemical sales.

2.4We will not ship chemistry through a system we cannot stand behind. A border is not a formality for a chemical product; it is a change of law, of labeling duties, of packaging standards, of who is answerable when a container fails. We could forward parcels across that line and call it shipping. We will not. When we can do it right, we will do it. Until then, we say no cleanly.

2.5A clean no is worth more than a careless yes. The label on every Product makes a promise about what is inside; that promise has to survive every hand and every mile between our facility and your address. Where we cannot guarantee that survival, we do not pretend to.

Article 3Handling Time

3.1Orders are prepared and handed to the carrier within 1–2 Business Days.

3.2Orders placed after 12:00 PM ET on Friday, or on a weekend or federal holiday, are processed the next Business Day.

3.3Handling time is preparation time: picking, inspection, closure check, packing to transport standard, and tender to the Carrier. It is separate from transit time, which begins when the Carrier takes possession and which no merchant controls directly.

3.4We will always tell you the truth about timing. If an Order cannot move within the promised window, see Article 8 — you are always given the choice to cancel for a full refund.

3.5The 1–2 Business Day window is the promise, not an aspiration. We would rather quote it honestly and hold it than quote faster and miss.

Article 4Ground-Only Chemical Transport

4.1All ALETH Products travel by Ground Service only. There is no expedited, two-day, overnight, or air option — for any Product, at any price.

4.2This is not an upsell in reverse. Consumer-scale chemical shipments move as Limited Quantity surface shipments under U.S. Department of Transportation rules (49 CFR 173.150); air transport of these goods is prohibited or restricted. The rule is federal. We simply follow it.

4.3Checkout enforces this: if your cart contains ALETH Products, Ground Service is the only method offered.

4.4We state the reason rather than hide behind a disabled button, because the reason is real and you are owed it. A Limited Quantity Shipment is permitted to travel on the ground in a consumer-scale container precisely because the conditions of surface transport — pressure, temperature, handling time — are bounded in ways that air transport is not. Altitude, vibration, and the compression cycles of an aircraft cargo hold change the physics around a sealed chemical container. The regulation draws the line where the physics draw it.

4.5So there is one way to move these goods correctly at this scale, and one way only, and we take it. Speed is not worth a container that arrives compromised. If Ground Service cannot get a Product to you on a schedule you can live with, the honest answer is your right to cancel under Article 8 — not a faster mode we refuse to sell.

Article 5Delivery Estimates & Risk of Loss

5.1Any delivery date or transit estimate shown at checkout or in your confirmation is an estimate, not a guarantee. Carrier performance is outside our control.

5.2Title to and risk of loss for a Product pass to you when we hand the package to the carrier.

5.3What that means in practice: while the package is ours, it is ours (Articles 8–10 cover loss and damage). Once the carrier has it, we fight for you — but we cannot promise a carrier's clock.

5.4An estimate is the honest form of a delivery promise when a third party's trucks, routes, weather, and volume are between us and your door. We publish estimates we expect to hold, we do not relabel them guarantees to close a sale, and when an estimate fails we do not hide behind the word "estimate" — Articles 8 through 10 say exactly what we do instead.

5.5The passage of risk at carrier handoff is the ordinary rule of a shipment contract. It is not an exit for us. The same Order that this Article moves past our door is the Order that Articles 9 and 10 stand behind if it is lost, damaged, or leaking — at our cost and our effort, not yours.

Article 6Shipping Charges

6.1Orders of $79 or more ship free via Ground Service within the Contiguous United States.

6.2Orders under $79 carry a standard Ground rate, shown at checkout before you pay. No surprise fees, ever.

6.3Free-shipping offers apply only to the Contiguous United States and only to Ground Service.

6.4The number on your screen before payment is the number. What you are quoted at checkout is what you are charged — no handling surcharge surfaced after the fact, no fuel adjustment appended to the receipt, no chemical-handling fee discovered on the invoice. If a charge is not shown to you before you pay, we do not add it afterward. That is what "no surprise fees, ever" means, and we hold ourselves to the plain reading of it.

6.5Because there is only one way to move these goods (Article 4), shipping is simple by construction: Ground Service, free at $79 or more, at a standard published rate below $79. There is no tier to decode and no faster tier to decline.

Article 7Delivery Addresses We Cannot Serve

7.1We cannot deliver to PO Boxes, or to APO/FPO/DPO military addresses. Chemical shipments move through commercial ground carriers that do not serve these addresses.

7.2Please supply a physical street address. If an Order is placed with an unserviceable address, we will contact you (Returns & Refunds Policy, Article 8).

7.3The reason is the same reason as everything else in this policy: what we can honestly deliver to, we deliver to, and what we cannot, we say so. A Limited Quantity Shipment cannot be dropped into a mail stream that was not built for chemical goods, and a military mail network is not a commercial ground lane. Sending a package toward an address that cannot lawfully receive it would create a shipment that fails in someone else's hands — yours, at the worst moment.

7.4If the address on an Order cannot be served, we contact you at the contact information attached to that Order and work the choice described in Returns & Refunds Policy, Article 8. We do not guess, re-route to a nearby address, or leave the outcome to a carrier's discretion.

Article 8Delays & Your Right to Cancel

8.1If we cannot ship your Order within the time promised (or within 30 days if no time was promised), we will contact you with a clear choice: consent to the delay or cancel for a full refund. Your silence is never treated as consent unless the law expressly permits it and our notice says so in those words. This is the standard of the FTC Mail, Internet, or Telephone Order Merchandise Rule, 16 CFR Part 435, and we hold to it in substance and in wording.

8.2If you cancel, your refund is issued promptly — within 7 Business Days — by a method at least as fast and reliable as the original payment. Not store credit. Not a coupon. Money back, by a route at least as good as the one it left by.

8.3If a revised shipping date slips again, we will ask you again. We do not hold your money hostage to our backlog.

8.4The choice is put to you in writing, at the contact information on your Order, in words a person can act on: the original date, what happened, the new date we now expect, and the two outcomes — wait, or be refunded in full. Consent, where it is given, is affirmative and recorded. Where consent is not affirmatively given and the law does not expressly permit silence to serve as consent, it is not consent, and the refund stands.

8.5A delay does not become your problem because time passed. Each new date is a fresh decision that belongs to you, not a continuation of an old one. And a full refund means a full refund: the price of the Product and the shipping you paid, by the faster of the two speeds you would accept — the method at least as fast and reliable as your original payment, within 7 Business Days of your cancellation.

8.6This Article exists because money in our account for goods that have not moved is money held against a promise we have not kept. That is not how we intend to run this company.

Article 9Lost Packages

9.1If your tracking shows no movement for 7 days or more, contact orders@alethchemical.com.

9.2We file the carrier claim ourselves — that is our job, not yours. Then we reship your Order or refund it, your choice.

9.3"No movement" means the tracking record has not advanced — no scan at a facility, no out-for-delivery scan, no delivery event — for the full 7 days or more. You do not need to diagnose the delay or prove the loss before we act. You tell us what the tracking shows; the claim work is ours to do.

9.4Claims are paperwork, hold times, and carrier procedures designed for shippers, not recipients. We are the shipper. We carry the claim from filing through disposition so that your involvement begins and ends with one email to orders@alethchemical.com.

9.5If the Carrier's record shows the package delivered but it is not in your hands, tell us at orders@alethchemical.com. Situations like that turn on facts — driver notes, address checks, delivery photographs — and we work those facts with the Carrier rather than leaving you to them alone.

9.6Whether we reship or you take the refund is your choice and yours alone. We do not steer it, and we do not condition one on the other.

Article 10Damaged or Leaking in Transit

10.1If your Order arrives damaged or leaking, contact orders@alethchemical.com within 7 calendar days of Delivery with photos of the package and the Product.

10.2We replace it or refund it — your choice — and we pay every cost. Full detail: Returns & Refunds Policy, Article 5.

10.3The photographs are not a hurdle; they are the evidence that settles the matter quickly. A shot of the outer package, a shot of the Product and its closure, and any shot that shows the damage itself are enough to open the claim and start your replacement or refund. Send what you have; if the images are unclear, we ask for better ones rather than closing the file.

10.4If a container is leaking, do not use the Product. Set it aside on a surface that will not be harmed, away from children and pets, and tell us at orders@alethchemical.com. What leaked out is not what the label promises, and what remains inside a breached container is no longer a Product we can vouch for.

10.5The 7 calendar days run from Delivery, the date the Carrier's record shows the package delivered to your address. Contact us inside that window and the replacement or refund is ours to fund, end to end: the Product, the shipping to return it if we ask for it back, and the shipping of any replacement. "We pay every cost" means every cost — you should never be out of pocket because a carrier handled our goods badly.

10.6A damaged delivery is a failure of the promise on the label, and the label is our oath. We would rather absorb the cost of making it right than argue about who broke it.

Article 11Taxes

11.1Sales tax is collected where required by law, and shown at checkout before payment.

11.2The tax line you see before you pay is the tax you pay. Where a jurisdiction does not require tax on a transaction, none is added; where it does, the amount appears at checkout before payment and no tax appears afterward that you were not shown.

Article 12Contact & Version

12.1Anything shipping-related: orders@alethchemical.com. A human answers.

12.2Material changes to this policy apply to Orders placed after the new effective date, never retroactively.

12.3The policy in force for your Order is the version published when you placed it. We do not move a term underneath an Order already in motion; where a change is material, it speaks only to Orders placed after the new effective date.

12.4One address, one promise: orders@alethchemical.com reaches people who can actually do something — open a claim, cancel an Order, replace a bottle, or answer why a rule exists. If your question is shipping-related in any way, that is the right address.
