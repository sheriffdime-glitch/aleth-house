## Article 2 — All Sales Are Final

**2.1 The rule.** All sales are final. ALETH does not accept returns or exchanges, and does not issue refunds, for change of mind, incorrect Product selection by the Customer, or dissatisfaction — whether the Product is sealed, unopened, or otherwise. This rule admits of no exception by age of the Order, by the condition of the Product, by the standing of the Customer, or by the size of the account. It does not soften with time and it does not bend for volume.

**2.2 The reason.** Once a Product leaves our custody, it does not come back. This is not a loophole or a formality. It is the foundation of everything we are, and Article 4 explains exactly why — in terms of chemistry, not paperwork.

**2.3 The promise.** Article 5 (Our Obligations) is the one exception, and it is not small — when the fault is ours or the failure is real, we make it right completely. Wrong item, Transit Damage, a Defective Product, a package that never arrives: you choose a full refund or a replacement, and ALETH pays every dollar of shipping in either direction.

**2.4 What "final" means in practice.** A final sale means the Order is complete when we accept it. There is no cooling-off period for chemical Products, no return window that opens after Delivery, and no restocking pathway. If you are uncertain which Product your application requires, write to **orders@alethchemical.com** before you order — a conversation before the sale costs nothing; a wrong selection after it cannot be undone.

**2.5** This Article applies to every sale made through alethchemical.com, including sales made under promotion (Article 10), and it applies equally to sealed, unopened, and unused Product. Article 5 governs where the failure is ours; everywhere else, this Article governs.

## Article 3 — Products That Never Return

**3.1** The following are non-returnable under any circumstances:

- (a) Any Product that has been opened, used, or partially used
- (b) Any Product whose Seal is broken, missing, or shows signs of tampering
- (c) Any Product that has been stored other than as directed on the label after Delivery
- (d) Custom, personalized, or made-to-order items
- (e) Gift cards
- (f) Items marked as final sale or promotional sale at the time of purchase

**3.2 Scope of the list.** Each entry in Article 3.1 is an independent bar. A Product within any one entry is non-returnable regardless of every other fact about it — regardless of whether the box is unopened, regardless of whether the Customer kept the receipt, regardless of how quickly the request is made, and regardless of whether the Product has ever been near a vehicle. Entries (a) through (c) describe conditions ALETH cannot verify in reverse (see Article 4); entries (d) through (f) describe Orders that were never returnable at the point of sale.

**3.3 This list is not negotiable case by case.** It protects every customer who buys from us after you. A single exception would put a container of unknown history back into commerce, and the cost of that is not measured in dollars.

**3.4 Physical returns are not accepted.** No Product may be shipped back to ALETH except under the return instructions issued in response to a Claim under Article 5. Chemical Products moved outside the hazmat chain are subject to the hazardous materials regulations of the United States Department of Transportation, including 49 CFR 173.150; a container sent to us unrequested, unpaid, or unannounced may be refused at the dock, quarantined, or destroyed without compensation, and ALETH is not responsible for it once it is abandoned in transit.

## Article 4 — The Chemical Integrity Doctrine

**4.1 Purpose and principle.** This Article states the principle on which ALETH's entire return policy rests, and it is the single most important clause in this document. ALETH Products are precision chemical formulations, engineered and blended to exact specifications of composition, concentration, and purity. Their fitness for use depends not only on how they were made, but on everything that happens to them after they leave our facility.

**4.2 The limits of verification.** Once a container passes into the hands of a carrier, a Customer, or any third party, it enters conditions that ALETH cannot observe, record, or reconstruct. Neither ALETH nor any subsequent holder of that container can establish whether the contents remain exactly as filled — whether the closure was opened and resealed, whether another substance was introduced, whether the Product was diluted, exposed to heat or freezing, or stored other than as the label directs. Chemical analysis cannot reliably distinguish a never-opened Product from a carefully resealed one. In chemistry, doubt is not a matter of paperwork. It is a matter of physics.

**4.3 The rule.** Accordingly, and without exception: no Product that has left ALETH's custody is ever returned to inventory, offered for sale, sampled, donated, or transferred to any person or entity for any purpose whatsoever. Every Product physically returned under Article 5 is quarantined on receipt, logged against its originating Order, and destroyed. ALETH absorbs that loss deliberately, absolutely, and permanently.

**4.4 The reason.** Our oath is truth in formulation. That oath means what the label says is inside the bottle is inside the bottle — for you, for the professional who trusts us with a client's vehicle, and for every customer who will never know how much discipline their bottle required. A single compromised container in commerce would cost more than its price. It would cost the word. ALETH refuses returns not from distrust of any Customer, but from respect for all of them — and for the chemistry itself.

**4.5 Why analysis cannot save us.** A laboratory can confirm that a sample matches a specification. It cannot confirm that the sample represents the whole container, that the container was never opened, or that nothing entered it and left no trace. Solvents evaporate and recombine. Surfactants degrade on a schedule that heat accelerates and cold disguises. A container can be drawn from, refilled to its original weight, resealed, and pass every casual inspection ever invented. The only control that has never failed is the one at the point of manufacture: our Seal, applied by us, under our observation, at our Premises. Every rule in this policy protects the integrity of that one fact.

**4.6 The quarantine protocol.** A Product physically returned under Article 5 is handled under a fixed procedure, without discretion: (a) it is received into a segregated quarantine area, never into saleable inventory; (b) it is logged against the originating Order number and Claim number, with the date of receipt and the name of the receiving handler; (c) it is destroyed within a reasonable period after the Claim is closed, by a method appropriate to its chemistry; and (d) the destruction is recorded. No step of this protocol may be skipped for any reason, including inspection, resale, staff use, charitable donation, demonstration, or sampling. "Destroyed" is what this document means, and destruction is exactly what happens.

**4.7 The economics we accept.** Every destroyed container is a container ALETH paid to formulate, fill, seal, pack, ship, receive back, and destroy. We know the arithmetic. We run it deliberately. The alternative arithmetic — one unknown container in a fleet of known ones — is a price we refuse to pay, and we would refuse it at ten times the cost.

**4.8 What this Article is not.** This Article is not an accusation. The overwhelming majority of returns anywhere in commerce are honest, and ALETH assumes the honesty of every Customer who writes to us. The rule exists because honesty cannot be demonstrated from the outside, and a chemical brand cannot afford to guess. Distrust of one Customer would be unfair; verification of none is the only standard that protects all.

## Article 5 — Our Obligations: Errors, Damage, Defects, Non-Delivery

**5.1 Covered events.** ALETH will make a full remedy, at our cost, when any of the following occurs:

- (a) The wrong item was shipped
- (b) The Order arrived with Transit Damage or leakage
- (c) The Product is Defective
- (d) The Order was never delivered (tracking shows loss or non-delivery)

**5.2 Your choice of remedy.** For any covered event, you choose one: a full refund to your original payment method, or a replacement shipped at our expense. The choice is the Customer's and not ours. Where a replacement is chosen, it ships as soon as the Claim is verified; where a refund is chosen, Article 6 governs timing. Store credit is available instead of either remedy on request (Article 6.3).

**5.3 We pay the shipping.** For covered events, ALETH pays all shipping costs — including return shipping where a return is requested. A prepaid label is provided on request. A Customer who pays a shipping cost to return a Product in a covered event will be reimbursed for it; no covered event costs the Customer a dollar to make whole.

**5.4 How to report.** Email **orders@alethchemical.com** within **7 calendar days** of Delivery (or, for non-delivery, within 7 days of the end of the estimated delivery window) with:

- (a) Your order number
- (b) Photos of the Product, the Seal, and the packaging

**5.5 Review and response.** ALETH acknowledges a Claim promptly and decides it on the evidence supplied. We ask only for what the decision requires — the order number, the photographs, and occasionally one fact only you can tell us. Where the evidence shows a Covered Event, we do not require the Product back before refunding or replacing unless we ask for it under the Article 4.6 quarantine protocol, and we pay the freight when we do. Where a report arrives after the 7-day window, ALETH will still hear it and decide it on its facts; the window is our reporting standard, not a trap, and the covered remedy does not evaporate at day eight.

**5.6 No forms. No arguments.** If it is ours to fix, we fix it — that is the whole policy.

**5.7 Non-delivery and shipment obligations.** Where an Order is not delivered, ALETH will trace it with the Carrier and resolve the Claim in the Customer's favor on confirmed loss, consistent with our obligations under the FTC Mail, Internet, or Telephone Order Merchandise Rule, 16 CFR Part 435, and the UCC Article 2 as enacted in North Carolina (N.C.G.S. Ch. 25, Art. 2). A tracking record showing delivery is evidence, not verdict: we investigate stolen-porch, misdelivery, and carrier-error cases on their facts (see Article 7.2).

**5.8** Our remedies under this Article are in addition to, and never in place of, any rights you have under applicable law that cannot be excluded (see Article 11).

## Article 6 — Refund Mechanics

**6.1** Once our review of a covered event completes, refunds are issued within **5 Business Days**. The five Business Days run from the completion of the review, and we complete reviews as fast as the evidence allows — most are decided on the day the Claim arrives.

**6.2** Refunds go to the original payment method. After we issue the refund, bank posting typically takes 3–10 business days and is outside our control. ALETH cannot accelerate a bank's posting cycle, cannot redirect a refund to a different card or account, and cannot split a refund across methods; the original payment method is the only channel, because it is the only channel with a verifiable path back to the money's source.

**6.3 Store credit is available instead of a refund on request.** Where a Customer prefers store credit — for speed, for convenience, or for a planned reorder — we issue it in the full amount of the refund that would otherwise be paid, including original shipping charges under Article 6.4. Store credit is issued to the Customer's account record and does not expire. It is a choice offered in the Customer's favor, never a substitute imposed by ALETH.

**6.4** For covered events, original shipping charges are refunded along with the Product price.

**6.5 Partial shipments and multi-item Orders.** Where an Order ships in more than one package, the 7-day reporting window under Article 5.4 runs from Delivery of the affected package. A refund in a covered event covers the affected Product and its share of shipping; the rest of the Order stands as sold.

**6.6 Currency and rounding.** Refunds are issued in United States dollars in the amount actually charged, to the cent. Where a payment processor imposes rounding or fees on the original transaction, the refund mirrors the charge as processed.

## Article 7 — Shipping Risk & Protection

**7.1** ALETH does not sell shipping-protection add-ons or paid insurance upsells. We cover loss and Transit Damage ourselves under Article 5. Risk transfer is not a product we sell. There is no checkout toggle, no premium tier, and no fee that buys a Customer what Article 5 already gives every Customer for free: our obligation to make it right when the shipment fails.

**7.2** Theft after confirmed Delivery is outside our control. We will help you file the carrier claim, and we will decide each case individually — fairly, and in your favor wherever the facts allow. Where tracking shows Delivery and the Customer reports non-receipt, ALETH will seek the Carrier's delivery record, geo-data, and scan history, and will weigh the Claim on the whole record rather than on the tracking line alone.

**7.3** Once a package is recorded as delivered, responsibility for it at the delivery location passes to you — from that moment it is the delivery location's security, not the Carrier's custody, that governs. Title and risk of loss passed earlier, at carrier handoff (Shipping Policy 5.2). This does not narrow Article 5: non-delivery, misdelivery, and Transit Damage remain Covered Events, and Article 7.2 governs the hard case in between.

## Article 8 — Address Errors & Re-Shipment

**8.1** If an Order cannot be delivered because the address supplied at checkout was incomplete or incorrect, we will contact you. ALETH uses the address exactly as the Customer supplies it at checkout; we do not correct, complete, or second-guess it, because a chemical shipment guessed into the wrong hands is worse than one delayed.

**8.2** Re-shipment to a corrected address is at the Customer's cost. The cost charged is the actual cost of the new shipment; ALETH adds no handling fee to it.

**8.3** If you choose not to re-ship, we will refund the Product price, less the original shipping charges already incurred. The deduction is the shipping already spent in attempting the delivery the wrong address prevented — nothing more is withheld, and no restocking fee exists.

**8.4 Where the error is ours.** If the Order cannot be delivered because ALETH or the Carrier misaddressed it — a warehouse picking error, a label printed against a different Order — then the address error is a Covered Event under Article 5.1(a), and Articles 5.2 and 5.3 apply in full: the Customer chooses a refund or a replacement, and ALETH pays all shipping in every direction. Articles 8.2 and 8.3 apply only where the address error is the Customer's.

**8.5 Packages returned by the Carrier.** Where a package returns to us as undeliverable, refused, or unclaimed through no fault of ALETH, Articles 8.2 and 8.3 govern, and the Product itself remains subject to Articles 3 and 4 — it does not return to our inventory in any case.

## Article 9 — Exchanges

**9.1** ALETH does not process direct exchanges. Where a remedy applies under Article 5, the path is refund or replacement — your choice under Article 5.2.

**9.2** An exchange in the ordinary sense — send one Product back, receive a different one — cannot exist under this policy, because no Product that has left our custody can re-enter ours (Articles 3 and 4). The nearest equivalent is a refund under Article 5 followed by a new Order; where the new Order is the result of a Covered Event, the refund path is the one described above, and the new Order is placed at the prices then published.

**9.3** A Customer who selected the wrong Product is not exchanging it under any construction — that selection error is governed by Article 2.1, and the sale is final.

**10.1** Orders placed under a promotion marked "final sale" at checkout are non-refundable except where Article 5 applies or applicable law requires otherwise. **10.2** Where a promotional Order qualifies under Article 5, the refund remedy returns the amount actually charged, and the replacement remedy ships the same Product at ALETH's expense. Promotional restrictions limit the price, never the remedy.

**10.3** Nothing in this Article limits rights under the North Carolina Unfair and Deceptive Trade Practices Act (N.C.G.S. Chapter 75) or any other consumer protection law that applies to the sale; see Article 11.

**11.6 Product warnings.** Cautionary labeling, including any warning provided under California Proposition 65, is required product information. A Prop 65 warning is not a defect, not damage, and not a Covered Event; the Product is not Defective under Article 1.8 because its label tells the truth about what it is.
